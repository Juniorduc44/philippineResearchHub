# HeliaSol façade film — one page

Adhesive organic-PV film (HeliaSol 436-2000) for walls and light roofs that cannot take silicon + racking.

**Do not** sell it as a better panel for a strong roof. Datasheet: ~50–55 W, 7.2–8.0% aperture efficiency, 1.6 kg, ±1600 Pa design load. That is manufacturer paper, not a typhoon certificate.

**Do** treat substrate (paint, corrosion, primer) and edge wind as the product.
