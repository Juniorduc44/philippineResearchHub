## What this build is
One sanitizing kiosk, one Cebu corridor (IT Park / JY Square), treated as the franchise prototype for later clones. Not a nationwide rollout.

## How the three books apply
- **Lean** — prove demand with one machine and counted cycles before buying a second. Village Laundry van analog.
- **E-Myth** — write how-we-do-it-here so the owner is not permanently the Technician with a spray bottle.
- **Profit First** — open the five accounts at 1% profit even at zero revenue; do not spend “all of the vendo cash.”

## What good looks like
Thirty days of real cycle logs at one site. Placement agreement on disk. Accounts named. Manuals that a substitute could follow.

## What to avoid
- Manufacturer payback stories as if they were this site’s numbers.
- Expanding to a second machine to “feel busy.”
- Mixing Cebu with Manila placement notes.
