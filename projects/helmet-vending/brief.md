# Brief

A self-service helmet sanitizing kiosk on the Cebu IT Park / JY Square corridor. Riders pay, leave the helmet in a chamber, get it back in minutes.

This project exists to **test that sentence at one bay**, not to become a helmet company.

Success for the prototype (E-Myth): a second unit could be stood up from the manuals without the founder living on-site.

Success for the experiment (Lean): 30 days of cycle counts that either support a second unit or kill/relocate this one.

Success for cash (Profit First): every peso through INCOME, never through a single mixed wallet.
