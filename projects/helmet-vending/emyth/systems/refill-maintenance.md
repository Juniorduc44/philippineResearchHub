# Refill and maintenance

**Purpose.** Cycles do not smell like an empty tank. Downtime is logged.

**Steps.**
1. Check solution level vs ml/cycle (once we have a real number).
2. Refill from labeled stock only. No mystery bleach.
3. If error: power cycle, then vendor phone, then bag the machine as “out” with a sign. Do not improvise chemistry.
4. Log downtime hours.

**Standard.** Consumable on-hand for 14 days of actual use, not of brochure use.
