# Placement

**Purpose.** Get a bay where a rider can stop, pay, and leave without blocking IT Park / JY Square flow.

**Steps.**
1. Walk three candidates (see `philippines/location-cebu-it-park.md`).
2. Note: power outlet, rain cover, sightline from security, motorcycle already stopping, competitor vendo.
3. Ask the owner: rent vs revenue share vs “leave it and we’ll see.” Save the answer as a file, not a memory.
4. Do not deliver a machine until electricity and a lockable-enough spot are real.

**Standard.** Written yes. Photo of the empty bay. Power tested.
