# Metrics

Lean (p.105, 113): if the growth engine does not move, product busywork is a vanity. Count behavior.

## Actionable (log these)
| Metric | Cadence | Why |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Paid cycles | daily | The product |
| Unique-ish repeats | weekly | H2 |
| Downtime hours | daily | H6 |
| Solution ml / cycle | weekly | Cost |
| kWh if the meter is readable | weekly | Cost |
| Cash vs QR split | 10th and 25th | Profit First rhythm |
| Site owner share paid | monthly | Real opex |

## Vanity (do not use to decide)
- Facebook views of a “we launched” post
- Manufacturer’s “₱30,000+/month” stories
- Helmets cleaned in some other province
- Followers, likes, “brand awareness”

Pivot trigger: 14 days under N cycles **and** a second bay is not already lined up → move or stop, do not “optimize the scent.”
