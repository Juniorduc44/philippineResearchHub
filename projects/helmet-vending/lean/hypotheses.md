# Leap-of-faith hypotheses

From Lean (this PDF p.57, 65): test **behavior**, not opinions. Each line is false until a log says otherwise.

| ID | Hypothesis | How we would know |
|----|------------|-------------------|
| H1 | Riders in this corridor will stop and pay to sanitize a helmet without an attendant | Paid cycles per day > 0 for 14 days |
| H2 | Delivery riders are the core repeat user, not BPO commuters | Repeat QR/coin pattern or simple tally of visor/delivery bags vs office clothes |
| H3 | ₱50 is a price they will pay at a parking/gas stop | Mix of ₱25 vs ₱50 vs ₱100 tests; watch cycle drop-off |
| H4 | A 6–10 minute cycle is short enough vs riding on | Queue of 0–1; complaints about wait |
| H5 | Estate/mall/gas will allow a 1 m² bay with power | Signed placement note on disk |
| H6 | One local machine at operator-brief capital can sit 30 days without a service truck | Downtime hours logged |

H5 is a gate. No machine purchase until a site says yes in writing (even a chat screenshot saved under `philippines/location-cebu-it-park.md`).
