# MVP: one machine, one bay, 30 days

Lean analog in the local scan: Village Laundry Services did not build a chain first. They parked a cheap setup to prove people would **bring laundry and pay** (PDF p.65). Zappos asked one question with a real transaction (p.57).

## The one question
Will motorcycle riders at **this** Cebu bay pay for a helmet cycle at least N times per day, for 30 days, without us standing next to the box?

N is unknown. Set N after three days of watching the empty bay (how many bikes already stop). Until then N = “enough that 10th/25th allocations are not a joke” — write the number here after day 3: `N = unknown`.

## What the MVP is
- One single-chamber unit (not dual, not a fleet)
- Weather cover
- Power that already exists
- Payment that we can reconcile (QR and/or coin)
- A paper or phone tally of cycles, downtime, and refill

## What the MVP is not
- A brand, an app, a second city, advertising screens, or “Go Clean but ours”

## Sequence
1. Shortlist 3 bays (`philippines/location-cebu-it-park.md`)
2. Placement yes
3. DTI name + Cebu LGU path started (`philippines/licenses.md`)
4. Buy or lease **one** machine against a dated quote
5. Run 30 days
6. Pivot or persevere (`learning-log.md`)

Ries (p.53): small real numbers beat a glamorous zero. Log ugly daily counts. Do not wait for a “launch event.”
