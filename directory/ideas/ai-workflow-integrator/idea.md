## One-liner

Philippine SMEs pay a setup fee, training, and a monthly care plan so Gmail or Workspace, spreadsheets, tickets, and a simple AI assistant actually talk to each other.

## Problem

- Who: Restaurants, hotels, cleaning companies, property managers, clinics — businesses with email + spreadsheet chaos
- Where: Cebu first; remote nationwide-digital after one local reference
- How the pain shows up: Fragmented digital workflows and AI adoption without technical staff. Reservation emails never become inventory. Guest messages never become tickets.

## Customer

- Segment: B2B operators who already pay for Gmail/Microsoft and do not have an engineer
- Region / city: Cebu first; remote nationwide-digital after one local reference
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Install/setup + staff training + monthly maintenance + workflow modifications. Examples in the operator brief: restaurant reservation→sheet→purchasing; hotel guest message→ticket; cleaning quote→schedule→invoice; property issue→technician→before/after photos.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Near-zero inventory. The operator already documents, trains, and automates (Python). The PH constraint is SMEs with tools they do not wire together — not “a growing AI market.”

## Model

- Revenue: Setup + training + monthly care + change requests
- Repeat vs one-time: High if the workflow stays in production
- Key cost: Labor, software seats (vendor prices unknown)

## Capital

- Band (PHP): Very low (operator-brief). Band (PHP): unknown
- Main uses of funds: Laptop, demo tenant, travel to the first five sites
