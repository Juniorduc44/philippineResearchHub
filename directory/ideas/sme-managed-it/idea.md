## One-liner

Hotels, clinics, restaurants, and small offices in Cebu pay a defined monthly retainer so someone who is not a full-time IT hire keeps Wi-Fi, PCs, backups, MFA, and CCTV running.

## Problem

- Who: Owners of hotels, dental clinics, restaurants, apartments, accounting offices, tour companies, property managers, small manufacturers
- Where: Cebu City first (IT Park / JY Square corridor as the walking radius); other LGUs unknown
- How the pain shows up: Broken Wi-Fi, no backups, shared passwords, unpatched PCs, CCTV that nobody watches, chaotic after-hours calls. Conversation contrast: charging ₱500 to reinstall Windows (operator-brief, not a market survey).

## Customer

- Segment: B2B MSMEs that cannot justify a full-time IT person
- Region / city: Cebu City first (IT Park / JY Square corridor as the walking radius); other LGUs unknown
- Ability to pay: unknown — operator-brief says price-sensitive; no invoice on disk

## Offer

- What they get: Productized packages from the operator brief: Business Digital Checkup (one-time); Business Protection (checkup + backup + MFA + password manager); Managed Business IT (monthly); Secure Business (keys + access + encrypted backups + device policy).
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026). Operator-brief only contrasts a ₱500 Windows reinstall. Do not treat that as the package price.

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: The operator already does electronics, networking, Linux/Windows, surveillance, and field service. Philippine MSME share cited in the conversation is **not** on disk — do not repeat the percentage.

## Model

- Revenue: One-time checkup fee + monthly retainer. Defined SLAs, not unlimited support.
- Repeat vs one-time: Recurring if the retainer holds
- Key cost: Labor hours, spare parts, software subscriptions (vendor prices unknown), transport

## Capital

- Band (PHP): Low (operator-brief). Band (PHP): unknown
- Main uses of funds: Laptop, toolkit, travel, first insurance/permit — not a data center
