## One-liner

Cebu server rooms, warehouses, and small factories pay a monthly fee for a simple sensor pack (temperature, humidity, leaks, smoke, power, doors) and a dashboard.

## Problem

- Who: Anyone with a closet that must not go silent: server rooms, cold storage, factories, warehouses, telecom rooms, future data halls
- Where: Cebu City first
- How the pain shows up: A leak, a trip, a door, or a hot rack that nobody sees until Monday. Power quality is a related later SKU (DOE grid-alert claims in the conversation are not on disk).

## Customer

- Segment: B2B facility / IT managers
- Region / city: Cebu City first
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Simple device + dashboard + monthly monitoring. Same family as cold-chain IoT, broader than food temperature. Later: power logging, UPS/generator, solar telemetry.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Electronics + networking + field service already in the bag. Industrial IoT is how a laptop business becomes infrastructure without owning the building.

## Model

- Revenue: Kit + monthly monitoring
- Repeat vs one-time: Very high
- Key cost: Sensors, connectivity, dashboard, call-outs
