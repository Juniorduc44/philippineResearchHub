## One-liner

Condo, BPO, factory, hospital, and hotel sites in Cebu pay (or share revenue) for unattended snack/drink machines that report stock, temperature, and sales.

## Problem

- Who: Site owners who want 24/7 retail without a cashier: condos, schools, gyms, call centers, factories, hospitals, hotels, transport nodes
- Where: Cebu City first; helmet-vending remains the one-machine sanitizing test on the IT Park / JY Square corridor
- How the pain shows up: A dumb machine that dies empty. Cash-only. No temperature on drinks. No sales log.

## Customer

- Segment: Site owners + end consumers
- Region / city: Cebu City first; helmet-vending remains the one-machine sanitizing test on the IT Park / JY Square corridor
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Distributed unattended retail: QR payments, remote inventory, temperature sensors, camera, sales analytics, low-stock alerts. Later: micro-markets. Distinct from `helmet-vending` (sanitizing service, not SKUs).
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026). Helmet-machine listings on disk are the wrong SKU.

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: The operator already studies unattended hardware (helmet kiosk). Adding telemetry is the technical advantage. Foreign-retail floors (RA 11595) may apply if this is classified as retail of goods — unknown until a lawyer/file says so.

## Model

- Revenue: Product margin + or vs site revenue share
- Repeat vs one-time: Daily if the site has traffic
- Key cost: Machine, product, restock labor, spoilage, site share, connectivity

- Band (PHP): Medium (operator-brief). Band (PHP): unknown per snack machine
- Main uses of funds: One snack/drink machine, first inventory, QR, telemetry, site deposit
