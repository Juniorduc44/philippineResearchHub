## One-liner

Households and small Cebu teams pay for a setup session: password manager, MFA, hardware keys, backups, and an account-recovery plan.

## Problem

- Who: Consumers who have been phished; businesses that need onboarding/offboarding
- Where: Cebu in person; remote nationwide-digital
- How the pain shows up: Account compromise, shared passwords, no offboarding, no recovery plan

## Customer

- Segment: Consumer package + business package (operator-brief)
- Region / city: Cebu in person; remote nationwide-digital
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Consumer: password manager, MFA, YubiKey, backup codes, encrypted backup, Proton/Gmail, phone security, recovery plan. Business: onboarding/offboarding, admin accounts, shared vault, device encryption, phishing training.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Operator already cares about YubiKeys, Proton, Google, secure documentation. The niche is being called before the hack, not after forensics (forensics licenses unknown).

- Repeat vs one-time: Medium (review) to high (business offboarding stream)
- Key cost: Keys (pass-through), time, training materials
