# Helmet sanitizing vendo (Cebu IT Park / JY Square)

## One-liner

Motorcycle and delivery riders in the Cebu IT Park / JY Square corridor pay per cycle to sanitize and dry a helmet in a self-service kiosk.

## Problem

- Who: daily motorcycle commuters, BPO shift workers, delivery riders, some bicycle riders
- Where: Cebu City — IT Park (Asiatown) and JY Square / JY Mall, Salinas Drive, Lahug
- How the pain shows up: sweat, odor, bacteria in a hot humid climate; shared or all-day helmets; manual pad-out cleaning is slow

## Customer

- Segment: (1) delivery riders serving the park/food strip (2) BPO/office motorcycle commuters (3) residents using JY Square as a stop
- Region / city: Cebu City, Lahug
- Ability to pay: operator-brief ₱25–₱100/cycle — **unverified**

## Offer

- What they get: unattended chamber cycle (fog/mist + UVC and/or ozone + dry + scent in most imported/local units described)
- Price band (PHP, year): ₱25–₱100 per cycle (operator-brief, 2026 notes). Confirm at site.

## Why Philippines

- Two-wheeler commuting and delivery are ordinary, not niche; humidity makes odor a weekly problem, not a luxury spa problem.
- Dwell exists where riders already stop (parking, gas, convenience, laundry).

## Model

- Revenue: pay-per-use (coin, bill, QR/card as the unit allows)
- Repeat vs one-time: repeat if the site has daily riders
- Key cost: machine, electricity, solution, site rent or revenue share, vandalism/maintenance

## Capital

- Band (PHP): operator-brief ~₱44,000–₱75,000+ for local basic to dual units; imports higher. **No invoice on disk.**
- Main uses of funds: one machine, weather cover, payment setup, first site deposit, solution stock
- Estimate year: 2026 notes

- Sector permits: operator-brief mentions “FDA-notified solutions” — **no FDA file on disk**. Electricity tap and mall/estate permission are practical blockers.
- Source: DTI start-grow library card; LGU unknown

## Location

- Region / city: Cebu City (IT Park / JY Square corridor)
- Site type: one protected bay with power, where a rider can stop without blocking flow — parking entrance, gas, convenience, or mall edge. Not “somewhere in Cebu.”

## Operations

- People: owner as Technician on refill/collection until a documented route exists (`projects/helmet-vending/emyth/`)
- Supply: sanitizing solution, spare parts, cash/QR reconciliation
- Hours / seasonality: 24/7 is the product if power and security hold; rain requires cover

## Competition

- Named PH players (operator-brief only): Go Clean (local), FresHelmet and similar imports; Cebu shops that detail helmet interiors by hand
- Gap: speed + unattended hours vs shop detailing — **unverified at this corridor**

## Risks

- Regulatory: LGU/estate permission; any device/solution claims (FDA) not evidenced here
- Supply / disaster: brownouts, typhoon, outdoor heat, vandalism
- Collection / demand: quiet site = no ROI; saturation if another vendo is already in the same parking
