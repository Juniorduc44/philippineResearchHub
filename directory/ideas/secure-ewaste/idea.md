## One-liner

Cebu offices and plants pay for documented collection of PCs, drives, and network gear: serial capture, wipe or shred, chain-of-custody, then authorized recycling for the hazardous remainder.

## Problem

- Who: Offices, hotels, plants, later industrial reverse logistics
- Where: Cebu City first
- How the pain shows up: Drives in a junk pile; WEEE in household trash; no paper trail for an audit

## Customer

- Segment: B2B asset owners who fear data leftover
- Region / city: Cebu City first
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Serial log, data wipe, drive destruction, chain-of-custody, reuse/refurb where safe. Hazardous WEEE through authorized channels. DENR WEEE claims in the conversation are not on disk — do not cite them as fact until ingested.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Hybrid of repair, privacy, and field logistics. Becomes reverse logistics for industrial electronics if the bench exists.

## Model

- Revenue: Per-asset or per-bin + certificate
- Repeat vs one-time: High for offices on a refresh cycle
- Key cost: Transport, wipe gear, shredder or vendor, authorized recycler fees, insurance

## Capital

- Band (PHP): Medium-high. Band (PHP): unknown
- Main uses of funds: Vehicle, cages, wipe station, shredder or partner, insurance
