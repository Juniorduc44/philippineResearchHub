## One-liner

Factories, hotels, and equipment owners in Cebu pay to repair industrial PCBs, power supplies, controllers, and displays instead of buying a new module.

## Problem

- Who: Plants, hotels, building automation, anyone with a dead controller that still has a mechanical body
- Where: Cebu / Visayas industrial and hotel stock first
- How the pain shows up: “Buy another expensive controller” when a board-level repair would do. Consumer phone repair is crowded; industrial is the claimed gap.

## Customer

- Segment: B2B maintenance managers
- Region / city: Cebu / Visayas industrial and hotel stock first
- Ability to pay: unknown — replacement cost is the ceiling, not a published PHP band

## Offer

- What they get: Board-level repair / 2M-style rework: industrial PCBs, PSUs, controllers, displays, comms equipment, legacy electronics. Documented return.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026). Conversation uses a foreign “$8,000 controller” as color — not a PH quote.

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Navy 2M / rework thinking is an operator skill. Consumer repair is crowded. As electronics production stays large in PH trade, a competent depot is useful — export share figures in the conversation are **not** on disk.

## Model

- Revenue: Per-job repair + possible depot contract
- Repeat vs one-time: Medium–high if plants send a stream of the same boards
- Key cost: Bench, microscope, parts, ESD, skilled hours
