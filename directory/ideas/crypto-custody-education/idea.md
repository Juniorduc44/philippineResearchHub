## One-liner

Adults in Cebu pay for classes on wallets, hardware-wallet setup, backups, scam identification, and recovery planning — with a hard line against holding, exchanging, or remitting crypto.

## Problem

- Who: Adults who want self-custody literacy; merchants who want education only
- Where: Cebu classroom / workshop; nationwide-digital for curriculum
- How the pain shows up: Scams, lost seeds, people asking a technician to “just hold it”

## Customer

- Segment: Education customers, not investors under management
- Region / city: Cebu classroom / workshop; nationwide-digital for curriculum
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Wallet education, hardware-wallet setup, backup planning, multisig education, Bitcoin/Lightning education, fee education, scam identification, merchant onboarding education, estate/recovery planning. Remittance comparison as software later — partner with licensed rails, do not become the rail.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Operator knows self-custody. Philippine regulation gets serious once you exchange, transfer, or safekeep. SEC CASP / BSP VASP items in the conversation are **not** on disk — treat education vs custody as a bright line until those files are ingested.
