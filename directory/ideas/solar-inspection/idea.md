## One-liner

Owners of existing rooftop arrays in Cebu pay for cleaning, visual/thermal inspection, inverter checks, and a simple performance comparison.

## Problem

- Who: Homes and SMEs that already have silicon on the roof; later warehouses
- Where: Cebu City / Metro Cebu roofs first
- How the pain shows up: Dirty modules, unnoticed inverter faults, nobody comparing output to last month

## Customer

- Segment: Existing-system owners, not greenfield EPC
- Region / city: Cebu City / Metro Cebu roofs first
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Cleaning, inspection, thermal inspection, connection inspection, inverter monitoring, performance comparison. Later pair with power monitoring and (separately) HeliaSol if that investigation proceeds.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026)

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Recurring, boring, uses field and electronics skills. Distinct from `heliasol-facade` (new OPV film, no ₱/W).

## Model

- Revenue: Per-visit + optional monitoring subscription
- Repeat vs one-time: High (dirt and weather come back)
- Key cost: Labor, water/access, IR camera, insurance, height safety

## Capital

- Band (PHP): Medium. Band (PHP): unknown
- Main uses of funds: IR camera, safety kit, cleaning tools, insurance
- Estimate year: 2026
