## One-liner

Cebu offices pay a monthly cleaning contract; the same company later sells data-center and ESD-sensitive cleaning to electronics rooms you would not mop like a hallway.

## Problem

- Who: Offices first; then facilities; then data-center rooms; then ESD/electronics environments
- Where: Cebu City first
- How the pain shows up: Offices need reliable night cleaning. Electronics rooms need a different SOP (chemicals, garments, floor). The conversation’s ₱300,000/month example is **hypothetical**, not a contract.

## Customer

- Segment: B2B facility managers
- Region / city: Cebu City first
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Progression: office → commercial/facility → data-center cleaning → ESD-sensitive → cleanroom support. The product becomes training, QC, SOPs, chemical control, ESD procedures, staffing, inspection — not the owner on a mop forever.
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026). Do not cite the conversation’s ₱300,000/month figure as a real contract.

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: Repeat customers, labor more than capex, contracts. Technology is the operational advantage (checklists, photos, sensors), not the mop. Pax Silica cleanroom work is a later rung.

## Model

- Revenue: Monthly contract per square meter or per room
- Repeat vs one-time: Very high if QC holds
- Key cost: Wages, chemicals, insurance, supervision, transport
