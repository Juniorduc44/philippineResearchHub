## One-liner

Restaurants, markets, pharmacies, and produce stores in Cebu pay a monthly fee for temperature, door, and power alerts on walk-in freezers and delivery boxes they already own.

## Problem

- Who: Anyone whose stock dies when a compressor dies: wet-market stalls, restaurants, pharmacies, fish/produce stores, delivery fleets
- Where: Cebu City first; DA food-hub claims in the conversation are **not** on disk
- How the pain shows up: Freezer #3 exceeds a safe band and nobody knows until the stock is lost. Power cuts. Door left open.

## Customer

- Segment: B2B owners of existing cold equipment — not a warehouse build
- Region / city: Cebu City first; DA food-hub claims in the conversation are **not** on disk
- Ability to pay: unknown

## Offer

- What they get: Sensor kit (temperature, humidity, door, power) + alerts + stored records. Distinct from `community-cold-storage` (a room you build).
- Price band (PHP, year): unknown (2026). Kit + monthly monitoring — no quote on disk

## Why Philippines

- Local constraint or advantage this idea uses: The hub already sourced post-harvest spoilage as a problem (`community-cold-storage` / Glacier interview). This idea sells telemetry to that ecosystem instead of competing with a DA warehouse.

## Model

- Revenue: Hardware margin (if any) + monthly monitoring
- Repeat vs one-time: Very high if alerts are trusted
- Key cost: Sensors, SIM/wifi, dashboard hosting, site visits
