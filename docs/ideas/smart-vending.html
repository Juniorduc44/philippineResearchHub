Smart vending / unattended retail

Do not think “vending machine.” Think distributed unattended retail: condos, schools, gyms, BPOs, factories, hospitals, hotels. Add QR, remote stock, temperature, camera, and low-stock alerts. Helmet sanitizing stays its own Cebu test. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 2 — recurring.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Boring on purpose. Recurring if the bay has people. The same remote-monitoring stack can later serve cold-chain and facility sensors.

For

  • Recurring product margin
  • Telemetry is the operator edge
  • Helmet kiosk already teaches unattended ops
  • Can grow into micro-markets

Against

  • Location is the business
  • Spoilage and vandalism
  • Possible foreign-retail floor if classified as retail of goods
  • No snack-machine invoice on disk

How practical

One indoor Cebu pantry with counted restocks for 30 days. Do not mix SKUs with the helmet sanitizing machine.

To take it up

  1. Pick one BPO or gym pantry with power and a gate guard.
  2. Quote one telemetry-capable snack/drink unit in PHP.
  3. Run 30 days of sell-through logs.
  4. Keep helmet-vending as a separate P&L.
