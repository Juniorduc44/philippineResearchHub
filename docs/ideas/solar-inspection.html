Solar inspection and O&M

Panels need cleaning, inspection, thermal checks, connections, inverter monitoring, and a performance comparison. You do not need to be a megawatt EPC. Later this can sit next to battery and cold-storage monitoring. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 2 — recurring.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Recurring work on hardware someone else already sold. Stays out of HeliaSol’s unquoted film until a distributor letter exists.

For

  • Recurring dirt and weather
  • Field + electronics skills
  • Pairs with facility monitoring
  • No container of modules

Against

  • Fall risk
  • Weather windows
  • Customers may ask for unlicensed electrical work
  • No PHP per-visit quote

How practical

Two-person rule and insurance before the first roof. Inspection report template in the project folder. Do not open an inverter you are not licensed to open.

To take it up

  1. Write a one-page inspection checklist.
  2. Price cleaning vs inspection vs thermal as separate SKUs.
  3. Do one paid house and one paid SME roof.
  4. Keep HeliaSol on its own investigation page.
