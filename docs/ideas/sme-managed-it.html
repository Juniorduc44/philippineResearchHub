SME managed IT + cybersecurity

Go into a hotel, clinic, restaurant, or small office and audit Wi-Fi, PCs, backups, MFA, CCTV, and accounts — then sell a written report and a monthly retainer. This is the cash-flow rung of the Traveling Techtician ladder. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 1 — cash.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

The operator already sits on electronics, fiber, Linux/Windows, networking, and surveillance. The product is defined packages (Checkup / Protection / Managed IT / Secure Business), not unlimited “computer repair.”

For

  • Skills already in the tool bag
  • Can start solo in Cebu
  • Monthly retainers possible
  • Remote support can sit on top of site visits

Against

  • Reputation is the funnel
  • SMEs can be price-sensitive
  • Unlimited support becomes chaos
  • NPC / DICT path not on disk

How practical

Philippine-national sole prop: DTI BNRS + Cebu City mayor’s permit + BIR. Sell defined SLAs. Foreign ownership of a small domestic-market shop may hit RA 11647 US$200k / US$100k floors — citizenship is an open question.

To take it up

  1. Write three package one-pagers with inclusions and hours.
  2. Walk five Cebu SMEs and sell one paid Checkup.
  3. Turn the Checkup into a retainer only after the report is delivered.
  4. Do not advertise unlimited support.
