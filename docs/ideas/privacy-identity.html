Privacy and digital identity

Password manager, MFA, hardware keys, backups, recovery plan. Business add-on: onboarding/offboarding, admin accounts, shared vault, phishing training. Become the person they call before somebody gets hacked. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 1 — cash.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Sits next to managed IT without needing a truck. Clear boundary: you set up identity; you do not become their password vault.

For

  • Very low capital
  • Feeds the MSP
  • Hardware keys are tangible
  • Training is already a skill

Against

  • Easy to undersell as “help me log in”
  • NPC/DPA claims need a file
  • Do not hold customer passwords
  • No Cebu price on disk

How practical

A two-hour consumer session product and a half-day business offboarding product. Written checklist in the repo.

To take it up

  1. Write the consumer checklist.
  2. Stock a small bag of hardware keys at pass-through cost.
  3. Sell five paid sessions.
  4. Refuse to store customer master passwords.
