Farm-to-market intelligence

There is room below national systems for tools serving cooperatives, traders, truckers, farmers, wholesalers, restaurants. Example: 14 tons of tomatoes harvest Friday in Region X — buyers see availability Tuesday. Inventory + logistics + marketplace + price discovery. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 3 — sensors.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Only after cold-chain sensors have real customers. Software without a field wedge is a ghost town. DA platform claims from the conversation stay uncited until ingested.

For

  • Fits the spoilage problem already sourced
  • Recurring SaaS if it sticks
  • Wedge from sensor customers
  • No warehouse capex

Against

  • Two-sided marketplace risk
  • DA overlap unknown
  • Slow co-op collections
  • Build cost in hours

How practical

Do not start here. Take cold-chain-iot customers and write down what they already WhatsApp. That list is the first spec.

To take it up

  1. Ingest DA AbCDE / food-hub pages if still pursuing.
  2. Collect 20 real WhatsApp harvest messages (permissioned).
  3. Ship a one-crop availability sheet, not an app store brand.
  4. Charge one trader group a monthly fee before building payments.
