Community cold storage

Shared, fee-per-crate cold room at the edge of a producing wet market. Cool first; freeze only if the crop and power plan support it. No LGU or PHP quote yet. The problem is now sourced from a local interview, not invented.

What the food-prices transcript says

Interview with a Glacier Cold Chain operator (Breaking Ground). His claims, not PSA tables. Full notes: research/briefs/2026-09-20-produce-cooling-from-transcript.md

  • Perishable harvest is dumped at isolated bagsakan (Bankerohan named) because growers are cut off from buyers.
  • Spoilage is passed to consumers as higher prices and worse produce.
  • The missing piece is quality preservation — cold storage near the farm and near cities (he names Manila, Cebu, Davao).
  • Blast freezing sits on the path to port/export, after storage.
  • If refrigeration or power dies, the stock dies. Backup generation is part of the model he describes.
  • A DA-built room in Benguet, in his account, became a dry warehouse once government kept operating it.

For (this scale)

  • Matches the farm-side gap he describes, not a 12,000-pallet plant
  • Gives growers a pause so they are not forced to dump
  • Cebu is already named as a demand-center city that needs rooms

Against

  • No crop, LGU, or kWh figure on disk
  • Brownouts / thin power reserves
  • Fee vs dump-sell price may still lose
  • His pallet-capacity numbers are not our business plan

To take it up

  1. Pick one crop and one producing LGU.
  2. Walk the bagsakan and note ice plants / DA posts.
  3. Price power + generator before the box.
  4. Copy the helmet-vending project inner layout once the site is real.
