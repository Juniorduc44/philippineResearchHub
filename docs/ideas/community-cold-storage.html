Community cold storage

Format specimen — not a researched filing. Shared, fee-per-crate cold room so smallholders near a wet market do not dump-sell harvest before the next buying day.

Why it sits in the gallery

Spoilage after a glut is a logistics problem, not a “farming tips” problem. The catalog keeps this as an example until one crop and one LGU are chosen. Do not treat placeholders as capital or license facts.

For (concept)

  • Matches fragmented smallholder plots + wet-market timing
  • Repeat fees through harvest calendar
  • Can sit as a cooperative or a private room

Against (until researched)

  • No LGU, crop, or kWh figure on disk
  • Power and typhoon risk
  • Growers may still dump-sell if the fee is high

To activate

Pick one crop and one municipality, then copy the inner layout from projects/helmet-vending/. Until then this folder is a shell.
