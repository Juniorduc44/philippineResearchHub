Commercial cleaning to ESD

Do not dismiss janitorial. Repeat customers, labor, contracts. The progression is office → facility → data-center → ESD electronics → cleanroom support. The owner’s job becomes SOPs, training, QC, chemical control — not the mop. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 4 — hire.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

A boring contract can fund the industrial rungs. The same company that learns night access in an office is easier to take into a server room later.

For

  • Recurring contracts
  • Labor more than machine capex
  • Ladder toward Pax Silica support
  • Checklists/photos fit the operator

Against

  • Payroll and no-shows
  • Theft risk
  • ₱300k/month in the brief is hypothetical
  • ESD/cleanroom credentials not on disk

How practical

Two Cebu office contracts with photo QC before any “semiconductor cleaning” claim. DOLE/SSS when you hire.

To take it up

  1. Write a one-page office SOP with before/after photos.
  2. Price two small offices.
  3. Hire only after the second contract is signed.
  4. Add an ESD annex only after a customer room exists.
