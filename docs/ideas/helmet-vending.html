Helmet sanitizing vendo

Self-service kiosk that sanitizes and dries a motorcycle helmet in a few minutes. This build is pinned to Cebu IT Park / JY Square (Lahug)—one corridor, one machine, a 30-day log. Not a nationwide brand.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Humidity plus all-day two-wheeler use makes odor a weekly problem, not a spa luxury. The first bet can stay small (operator notes: local units around PHP 44k–75k+; no invoice on disk). IT Park is a 24/7 BPO hub; JY Square is the adjacent gateway. Quality here means a real local pain and a cloneable test—not a brochure PHP 30,000/month.

For

  • Pain matches climate and motorcycle commuting
  • Unattended after placement; ~1 m²
  • Night-shift hours shops do not cover
  • Repeat use plausible for delivery riders

Against

  • Location is the business; a quiet bay dies
  • Vandalism, brownouts, rain, heat
  • Fog/UV is not pad-out detailing
  • Payback stories are arithmetic, not this site

How practical

Intended path: Philippine-national sole prop, DTI BNRS + Cebu City mayor’s permit + BIR, walk three bays, run one unit for 30 days. Not week-one passive income. Foreign equity would need EO 113 / RA 11647; RA 11595 (PHP 25M foreign-retailer floor) only if this is classified as retail of goods.

To take it up

  1. Walk three bays, day and a BPO night window.
  2. Get written placement (power + rain cover).
  3. Register, then buy or lease one quoted machine.
  4. Log cycles for 30 days before a second unit.
