Fiber, network, and CCTV installs

Start with office networking. Then hotels and condos. Then warehouses. Then industrial buildings. Services: fiber termination, testing, racks, structured cabling, access control, CCTV, and a binder of what you actually built. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 2 — recurring.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

This is one of the stronger Pax Silica bridges in the operator brief — and hotels still pay this year. Documentation and testing are the differentiator, not cheaper cameras.

For

  • Skills already present
  • Maintenance contracts after install
  • Feeds MSP and remote-hands later
  • Physical work that software shops skip

Against

  • Tooling capex (splicer/OTDR) unknown
  • NTC/PECE path unknown
  • Night hotel work
  • Copper theft

How practical

Rent test gear for the first three jobs. One hotel or office as-built before buying a fusion splicer.

To take it up

  1. List test gear to rent in Cebu.
  2. Price one office recable and one eight-camera job.
  3. Deliver as-builts as a PDF in the repo style.
  4. Add a yearly clean-and-check contract.
