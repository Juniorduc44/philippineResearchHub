Facility environmental monitoring

Sensor packs for temperature, humidity, water leaks, smoke, power failure, door access, vibration, air quality. Targets: server rooms, warehouses, factories, telecom closets. Simple device. Simple dashboard. Monthly fee. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 3 — sensors.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Starts very small and can become industrial IoT. Shares a stack with cold-chain monitoring without requiring a warehouse. Power-quality logging is a later add-on — DOE grid-alert articles from the conversation are not on disk.

For

  • Same skills as IT and electronics
  • Recurring
  • Feeds remote-hands later
  • No building ownership

Against

  • Alert fatigue
  • IT departments blocking devices
  • Fire-code claims would be a different business
  • No PHP kit quote

How practical

Ten paying closets on off-the-shelf sensors. Do not brand this as a fire-protection company.

To take it up

  1. Pick one off-the-shelf sensor family.
  2. Install in two friendly Cebu server closets.
  3. Measure false alerts for 30 days.
  4. Add power logging only after temperature/leak is trusted.
