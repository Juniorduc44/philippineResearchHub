Crypto self-custody education

Sell wallet education, hardware-wallet setup, backups, multisig literacy, scam identification, merchant onboarding education, estate/recovery planning. Do not hold coins, manage portfolios, exchange, or run remittance. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 1 — cash.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

The operator brief is explicit: education is interesting; custody/exchange/remittance is a regulatory business. SEC CASP and BSP VASP documents cited in the conversation are not ingested yet, so this page stays on the education side of the line.

For

  • Very low capital
  • Matches operator knowledge
  • Clear product boundary
  • Can later compare licensed remittance rails without becoming one

Against

  • Students may ask you to take custody
  • SEC/BSP files not on disk
  • Scam-loss blame risk
  • No class price on disk

How practical

A written “we never hold keys” agreement on every seat. Ingest SEC CASP and BSP VASP memos before any paid merchant-onboarding that looks like facilitation.

To take it up

  1. Draft the class outline and the no-custody contract.
  2. Ingest SEC MC 04-2025 and BSP VASP memos into the library.
  3. Run one paid six-person workshop.
  4. Refuse any request to hold seed phrases.
