Industrial electronics repair

Consumer phone repair is crowded. Industrial repair is the interesting job: PCBs, power supplies, controllers, displays, communication gear, legacy boards. The pitch is repair the board instead of replacing the whole controller. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 5 — industrialize.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

This is the operator’s Navy 2M background used as a business, not a hobby bench. It also feeds later Pax Silica “picks and shovels” (electronics maintenance around plants you do not own).

For

  • Skill is rare vs phone kiosks
  • High willingness to pay vs replacement
  • Depot can sit behind MSP customers
  • Documentation is already a habit

Against

  • Parts for obsolete gear
  • Unrepairable boards still cost time
  • Capex for a real ESD bench
  • No PH replacement-cost table on disk

How practical

One rented bench and three paying industrial jobs before a shop sign. Photograph and log every board.

To take it up

  1. List three Cebu plants or hotels with known controller brands.
  2. Quote a no-fix-no-pay diagnostic fee.
  3. Stand up ESD-safe bench photos for the page (generated art is not the bench).
  4. Refuse medical/safety devices until a permit file exists.
