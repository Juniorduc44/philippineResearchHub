Cold-chain IoT monitoring

You do not need to build a cold-storage warehouse first. Put sensors in walk-ins, fish boxes, produce rooms, trucks, restaurant coolers, and pharmacies. Alert: freezer #3 just left the band. Store the records. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 3 — sensors.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

The hub already sourced spoilage as a local problem. The Department of Agriculture claims in the conversation (mega food hubs, Cebu terminal) are not on disk — so this page does not repeat them as fact. The practical bet is telemetry for equipment that already exists.

For

  • No warehouse capex
  • Recurring monitoring fee
  • Pairs with later cold delivery
  • Electronics skill matches the kit

Against

  • False alarms kill trust
  • Food/pharma logger rules unknown
  • SIM/wifi in wet markets is ugly
  • DA hub claims not ingested

How practical

Ten kits on paying Cebu freezers before any custom PCB. Keep this legally separate from building a plant (`community-cold-storage`).

To take it up

  1. Buy ten off-the-shelf loggers and a dashboard.
  2. Install on one restaurant and one market stall that already pay for power.
  3. Log 30 days of alerts vs missed events.
  4. Only then talk custom firmware.
