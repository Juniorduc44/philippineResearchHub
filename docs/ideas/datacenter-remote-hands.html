Data-center remote hands

Customers need someone physically present to swap drives, reseat cables, reboot, photograph indicators, patch fiber, and rack gear. They do not need to fly an engineer to the Philippines. They need a trusted technician. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 5 — industrialize.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

This is the industrial version of the MSP. It only works with access and trust. Generated photos are not a badge.

For

  • Matches the skill mix
  • Retainer + dispatch
  • Feeds fiber and electronics repair
  • Works in closets before true hyperscale halls

Against

  • Access is gated
  • Pax Silica halls are not a 2026 paycheck (timing not on disk)
  • Liability
  • No dispatch rate on disk

How practical

Start as after-hours hands for existing Cebu MSP customers’ server closets. Collect photo SOPs. Bid on a colo roster only with insurance.

To take it up

  1. Write a remote-hands SOP (photo, ticket, two-person for destructive steps).
  2. Price after-hours closet visits for current IT customers.
  3. Get insurance quotes (unknown until asked).
  4. Do not market a Clark hall you cannot enter.
