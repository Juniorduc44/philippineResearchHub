Secure e-waste and data destruction

Collect PCs, servers, drives, phones, network gear. Document serials. Wipe or destroy. Chain-of-custody. Send actual hazardous WEEE through authorized channels. This can become reverse logistics for industrial electronics. Photos are generated portfolio stills, not a paying Cebu site. No PHP quote on disk. Ladder: Phase 5 — industrialize.

Why it may be a quality opportunity

Sits on privacy + repair + field work. DENR language from the conversation is not ingested, so this page does not pretend a permit exists.

For

  • Audit story for customers
  • Feeds the repair depot
  • Recurring office refresh
  • Industrial later

Against

  • Hazardous-waste permits unknown
  • Liability if a drive leaks
  • Recycler quality
  • No PHP per-drive quote

How practical

Partner with a named authorized recycler before the first pickup. Dual-control log. No household junk route.

To take it up

  1. Find and ingest a DENR/EMB WEEE page.
  2. Name one authorized Cebu/Visayas recycler.
  3. Pilot ten office drives with certificates.
  4. Refuse undocumented walk-in junk.
