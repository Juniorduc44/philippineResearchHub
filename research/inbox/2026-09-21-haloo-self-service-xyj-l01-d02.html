Product Description

 

 

With the continuous expansion of shared mobility, electric motorcycles, food delivery, and short distance rental industries, helmets have become high-frequency reusable public equipment. However, during high-frequency circulation use, sweat stains, odors, bacteria, and particulate pollutants are prone to accumulate inside the helmet, leading to a decrease in user experience and increasing the hygiene management pressure on the operator.

 

Haloo smart helmet cleaning vending machine is designed specifically for unmanned scenarios, integrating cleaning, deodorization, sterilization, drying, and dust removal functions. Through a self-service commercial operation model, it provides standardized and efficient helmet hygiene solutions for shared helmet operators, rental points, and cultural tourism cycling service scenarios.

 

Industry pain points: Why is it necessary to upgrade professional cleaning solutions?

 

In the shared helmet or public rental model, operators generally face the following problems:

 

1. The helmet produces a stubborn odor after long-term use, which affects the user's use
2. The manual cleaning process is complex and the cost continues to rise
3. Low cleaning efficiency leads to a decrease in equipment turnover speed
4. Difficulty in implementing unified hygiene standards and difficulty in management
5. Increased risk of bacterial and fungal growth in humid environments
6. Insufficient trust of users in the hygiene of public helmets

7. These issues directly affect user retention and operational revenue.

 

Haloo standardizes and processes helmet hygiene management through automated cleaning and intelligent control systems, fundamentally addressing operational pain points.

 

 

Core Technology Highlights

 

▶ 360 ° aerosol circulation purification system | deep decomposition of odors

 

Traditional spray or surface cleaning methods can only handle outer layer pollution and cannot penetrate deep into the helmet lining structure.

 

Haloo adopts 360 ° circulating aerosol purification technology, which spreads the cleaning medium evenly throughout every corner of the helmet through a fully enclosed space.

 

Core advantages:


• Penetrating the inner fiber layer to achieve deep purification
• Decompose sweat, smoke, and long-term accumulated odors
• Keep clean and dry after cleaning
• Significantly improve wearing comfort

 

This technology is particularly suitable for high-frequency helmet usage scenarios, such as food delivery riders and shared cycling services.

 

▶ Efficient negative pressure dust removal system | Building a clean internal environment

 

Helmets are prone to dust, hair debris, and air pollution particles during circulation, which are often difficult to completely remove through ordinary cleaning methods.

 

The device is equipped with a high-strength negative pressure adsorption module, which achieves rapid removal of internal pollutants through directional airflow circulation.

 

System features:

 

Strong adsorption of fine dust and particulate pollutants
Remove hair and fiber residue
Optimize the air cleanliness inside the helmet
Improve overall cleaning efficiency and consistency

 

Combined with the air circulation purification structure, it effectively reduces the problem of secondary pollution during the cleaning process.

 

▶ Dual cleaning mode system | Flexible adaptation to different operating environments


The device supports two standardized cleaning solutions, which can be flexibly switched according to different operational scenarios:


● Distilled water cleaning mode


Adopting a high-purity water source system effectively reduces residual impurities:


No mineral deposition risk
Avoid scale and secondary pollution
Better protect the lining material structure
Improve overall cleaning stability


● Professional disinfectant mode


Suitable for high-frequency circulation or high hygiene requirements scenarios:


Enhance sterilization ability
Enhance the level of public health safety
Meet the requirements of different national health standards


This dual-mode design provides the device with stronger market adaptability and operational flexibility.
