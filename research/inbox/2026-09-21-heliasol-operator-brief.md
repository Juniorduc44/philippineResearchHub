# HeliaSol operator brief

Hub conversation (2026-09-21). **Not a manufacturer invoice.** Specs that appear here must be re-checked against `research/library/2026-09-21-heliatek-heliasol-436-2000-datasheet.md`.

Position: solar for buildings that could not have solar before — lightweight roofs and large façades — not a ₱/W fight against silicon on a strong roof.

First experiment proposed: 10–20 films on galvanized, painted steel, aluminum, and primed concrete; log temperature, power, irradiance, edges, corrosion through dry season, monsoon, and one storm season.

Adhesive concern reframed: heat/humidity are in Heliatek’s IEC/climate tests; PH failure mode more likely paint/corrosion/typhoon peel at edges.
