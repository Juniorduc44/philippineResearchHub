Philippine Standard Industrial Classification

  1. Home >
  2. Statistical Classification Systems >
  4. Philippine Standard Industrial Classification

The Philippine Standard Industrial Classification (PSIC) is the country's official framework for classifying establishments and enterprises according to their primary economic activity. PSIC Revision 5 is patterned after the United Nations International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities (ISIC) Revision 5, with modifications to reflect the country's economic structure, requirements, and evolving economic activities.

The PSIC Revision 5 was developed to:

1) respond to changes in economic activities, including the emergence of new industries and developments in existing industries; 
2) account for technological advancements that affect the organization of production and the shifting of economic activities; and 
3) maintain alignment with the latest ISIC revision to promote the international comparability of economic statistics.

As the country's framework for classifying economic activities, the PSIC facilitates the consistent collection, processing, compilation, and dissemination of industrial statistics. It also provides a common basis for producing comparable statistics across government agencies, private sector entities, and other organizations engaged in statistical and research activities. In addition, it supports the integration of statistical data from various sources for economic analysis, planning, and policy formulation.

The PSIC Revision 5 presents the hierarchy of industrial categories through sections, divisions, groups, classes, and sub-classes. It also provides a detailed classification showing the complete structure of industries, consisting of:

22 sections (one-alpha code) 
88 divisions (two-digit codes) 
260 groups (three-digit codes) 
493 classes (four-digit codes) 
1,338 sub-classes (five-digit codes)
