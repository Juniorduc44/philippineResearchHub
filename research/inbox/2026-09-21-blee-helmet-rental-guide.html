Helmet Self-Service Cleaning Machines for Delivery & Rental Businesses

Blee Arcade’s helmet self-service cleaning machines offer an efficient solution for delivery companies, courier services, and helmet rental businesses.
Maintaining hygiene is crucial when helmets are shared among multiple users or employees. Our machines provide fast sterilization, odor removal, and drying, ensuring helmets are safe and ready for immediate use.

Applications in Delivery & Rental Services

  • Delivery & Courier Hubs: Keep helmets clean for riders in high-traffic delivery operations.
  • Helmet Rental Services: Provide hygienic helmets for multiple customers in rental or sharing programs.
  • Corporate Fleets: Maintain safety and hygiene for company-provided helmets.
  • Shared Mobility Programs: Ensure a safe and pleasant experience for scooter or motorcycle sharing users.

Key Benefits for Businesses

  • Reduces health risks and increases customer trust.
  • Automated operation minimizes labor costs.
  • Fast 3–5 minute cleaning cycle keeps helmets ready for immediate use.
  • Flexible payment: coins, QR codes, or membership cards.
  • OEM/ODM customization: branding and interface for your business.

How It Works

  1. Insert the helmet into the machine.
  2. Activate the cleaning process via coins, QR code, or membership card.
  3. UV-C sterilization and ozone system disinfect the helmet.
  4. Hot air drying ensures the helmet is ready for use.
  5. Automated signals indicate completion, ready for pickup.

FAQ

1. Can the machine handle multiple helmets quickly?

Yes, with a 3–5 minute cleaning cycle per helmet, multiple machines can serve high-volume operations efficiently.

2. Is it suitable for shared or rental helmets?

Absolutely. Designed for shared use, ensuring hygiene and odor-free helmets for every customer.

3. What payment methods are supported?

Coins, QR codes, and membership cards are all supported for convenient self-service.

4. Can the machine be customized for my business?

Yes, Blee Arcade provides OEM/ODM options including branding, interface, and color customization.
