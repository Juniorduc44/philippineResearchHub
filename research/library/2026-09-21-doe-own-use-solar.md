## What this is
Department of Energy (Philippines) source ingested 2026-09-21 for the HeliaSol façade-film idea.

## Claims worth citing
- Media release 20 August 2026. Department Circular DC2026-08-0017 streamlines Self-Generating Facility Zero-Export Solar Systems (SGF ZESS) and Micro-Solar Systems (MSS).
- MSS: consumer-grade plug-and-play up to 1 kW; no Building Permit or ERC COC; notify the distribution utility.
- Qualifying SGF ZESS in homes/clinics/hospitals: COC waived; Building Permit not required where the structure already has applicable clearance.
- DUs directed to remove unnecessary pre-installation clearances and inspection charges as connection conditions.

## Limits
Policy for own-use zero-export and micro-solar. A warehouse façade string may still be a larger SGF / net-metering case — do not assume MSS rules cover a 10 kWp wall.
