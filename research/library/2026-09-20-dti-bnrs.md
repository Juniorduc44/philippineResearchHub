## What this is
Public BNRS portal for sole-proprietor business names: search, new registration, renewal, cancellation, certification.

## Claims worth citing
- A Business Name is any name different from the individual’s true name used on receipts, agreements, or signage.
- Services on the portal: Search, New Registration, Renewal, Cancellation, Request Certification, Transaction Inquiry.
- Helpdesk: bnrshelpdesk@dtiphmail.dti.gov.ph; Text/Viber 09175953930 (as of fetch date).

## Limits
This is the app shell, not the fee table or the legal circular. Do not invent registration fees from this file.

## Quote
> Business Name (BN) shall refer to ANY name that is different from THE TRUE NAME OF AN INDIVIDUAL WHICH IS USED OR SIGNED in connection with her/his business on any written or printed receipts…
