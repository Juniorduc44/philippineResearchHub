## What this is
RA 11595 (10 December 2021), amending RA 8762. Lowers foreign-retailer capital.

## Claims worth citing
- Foreign retailer minimum paid-up capital: **₱25,000,000**.
- More than one physical store: **₱10,000,000** minimum investment per store (with grandfathering).
- Reciprocity: origin country must not bar Filipino retailers.
- Maintain the ₱25M in the Philippines; BSP inward-remittance cert (or bank proof) at registration.
- SEC monitors partnerships/corps; DTI monitors sole props.
- DTI + SEC + NEDA review the floor every three years.

## Helmet-vendo note
This Act is about **foreign-owned retail of goods**. It is not a Cebu mayor’s-permit manual. Use it only if the vendo is (a) classified as retail and (b) has foreign equity.
