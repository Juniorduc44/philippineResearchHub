## What this is
7-page scan of EO 113 (13 April 2026), promulgating the 13th RFINL. Image PDF (ApeosPort copier). Text is OCR.

## Claims worth citing
- RA 7042 as amended by RA 8179 and **RA 11647** is the statute behind the list.
- List A item 14: retail trade enterprise with paid-up capital **less than ₱25,000,000** — foreign equity capped at 40% (cites RA 11595).
- Footnote 7 restates RA 11595: ₱25M paid-up; reciprocity; ₱10M per extra physical store.
- List B items 6–7: micro/small **domestic market** enterprises below **US$200,000** paid-in equity reserved to Philippine nationals; **US$100,000** if advanced tech / Innovative Startup Act endorsement / ≥15 Filipino employees (majority Filipino).

## Helmet-vendo note
A Filipino-owned sole prop is not trying to take foreign equity. FINL and RA 11595 bite if a **non-Philippine national** invests, or if the activity is classified as retail of goods. A pay-per-use sanitizing **service** is not automatically “retail trade” under RA 8762/11595 — that classification is still open.
