## What this is
RA 11647 (2 March 2022), amending RA 7042. This is the statute EO 113 implements via the 13th RFINL.

## Claims worth citing
- Non-Philippine nationals may own up to 100% unless a law or the Negative List says otherwise; SEC/DTI must not add extra ownership caps.
- Micro and small **domestic market** enterprises under **US$200,000** paid-in equity are reserved to Philippine nationals, with a **US$100,000** door if advanced technology / startup endorsement / ≥15 Filipino staff (majority Filipino).
- Points to RA 8762 for retail.

## Helmet-vendo note
If the owner is a Philippine national and capital is local, this Act is background. It becomes operational if foreign money is invited into a small domestic-market kiosk.
