## What this is
DTI Business Center page on starting and growing a business. Best local source so far for which agency a new PH enterprise registers with.

## Claims worth citing
- Sole proprietors register a business name with DTI (walk-in at regional/provincial offices or online at bnrs.dti.gov.ph).
- Partnerships and corporations register with SEC (head office listed as 7907 Makati Avenue, Salcedo Village, Bel-Air, Makati City, 1209).
- Cooperatives register with CDA under RA 6938/6939.
- All legal forms need a mayor’s permit from the LGU; procedure varies by city/municipality (DILG).
- Every enterprise registers with BIR for TIN and authority to print receipts.
- Employers register with SSS. DOLE registration is required for firms with 50 or more workers; encouraged at 5+.
- BMBEs may register free at Negosyo Centers; DTI issues the Certificate of Authority under DAO 16-01 (22 April 2016). Listed incentives include income-tax exemption on BMBE operations, minimum-wage-law exemption, and a special credit window — **verify current law before using these in an idea file**.

## Limits
Phone numbers, office addresses, and BMBE incentive language can go stale. DAO 16-01 is 2016. Treat this page as the registration map, not a fee schedule. Generic “how to write a business plan” copy on the same page is not PH-specific evidence.

## Quote
> In putting up a business, there are several government agencies you have to register with depending on the type of your business.
>
> It is necessary to register your business with this agency [DTI] to gain the exclusive rights to use your business name. Single proprietors are required to register.
>
> All businesses, whatever the legal form, are required to secure a mayor’s permit or municipal license from the city or municipality where they are located.
