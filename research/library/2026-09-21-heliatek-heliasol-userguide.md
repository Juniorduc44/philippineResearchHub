## What this is
Heliatek GmbH source ingested 2026-09-21 for the HeliaSol façade-film idea.

## Claims worth citing
- Mounting surface must have inclination min. 1° and max. 90° (façade) so water drains.
- Substrate must be solid, load-bearing, free of damage, corrosion, or degradation; sandy plaster, paint flaking, or corrosion are not suitable.
- IEC 61730 & 61215 certification tests performed on metal substrate; additional substrates climate-chamber tested for adhesion.
- Professional/authorized specialist installation per the guide, not DIY.

## Limits
Manufacturer install rules. Not a PH wind-load study. Remaining roof/façade service life of 20 years is a Heliatek requirement, not a local code citation.

## Quote
> The mounting surface on which HeliaSol is fixed must have an inclination of min. 1° and max. 90° (façade). Sandy substrates (e.g. old plaster façades) or surfaces with paint flaking or corrosion are not suitable.
