## Question
Do EO 113 / RA 11647 / RA 11595 change how we register a Cebu helmet sanitizing kiosk?

## What the local files say
- **Philippine-national sole prop, local capital:** still DTI BNRS + Cebu LGU + BIR. These three statutes are not the permit path.
- **Foreign equity in a small domestic-market business:** RA 11647 / EO 113 List B — below US$200,000 paid-in is reserved to Philippine nationals (US$100,000 with the tech/startup/15-Filipino exceptions).
- **Foreign-owned retail of goods:** RA 11595 / EO 113 List A item 14 — ₱25M paid-up; ₱10M per extra store; 40% cap if paid-up is under ₱25M.
- **Is a helmet vendo “retail”?** The Acts talk about retail trade enterprises and stores. A pay-per-use sanitizing **service** is not named. Do not assume RA 11595 applies. Do not assume it does not, if DTI/SEC later call the activity retail.
