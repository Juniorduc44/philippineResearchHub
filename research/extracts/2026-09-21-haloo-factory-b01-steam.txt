Product About Helmet Cleaning Machine
Helmets are important equipment for outdoor cycling
In the bustling streets where cycling culture thrives, are you bothered by the hygiene issues of your helmet? This helmet cleaning vending machine is designed to solve this problem – 1-minute dust removal + UV sterilization, eliminating stubborn sweat stains and odors with a single touch. No need to wait for store service; it's readily available at subway stations, campuses, and cycling clubs. Simply scan the QR code to pay and enjoy a clean experience, ensuring safety and freshness at all times.

Main Process
1. Place the helmet and close the door
2. Dust removal and sterilization
3. spray disinfection: atomized spray
4. Steam mite removal: Steam at 70-80 degrees Celsius forms mist after heating the disinfectant to remove bacteria and mites
5. Drying, deodorization, hot air blowing: PTC electric heating, heating temperature up to 70-80 degrees (similar to blowing hair)
6. Spray fragrance and antibacterial properties 7. Remove the helmet and close the door
Product Introduction
This single door helmet disinfection machine is suitable for various scenarios with a reasonable size of 660W * 1966H * 600D. It is equipped with a dual disinfection and sterilization system of steam and ultraviolet light, and with PTC heating and drying technology, three cleaning modes can be switched as needed. The 5L disinfectant bin ensures long-term work, disinfection and drying in one step, so that the helmet is always dry and sterile. Four 450ml perfume bottles provide three fragrance options. After disinfection and sterilization, they bring fresh air and improve the use experience.
The device is equipped with a 12.1-inch touch screen, which is intuitive and convenient to operate. It supports WiFi/4G dual network mode and dual payment systems for card swiping and coin insertion. The Android system is stable and smooth, with a magnetic lock to ensure security, and a SaaS IoT cloud platform for remote management. From monitoring the operating status to parameter adjustment, the entire process is worry free. With a rated power of 2100W, it efficiently meets various helmet disinfection needs.
Product Core Value
1. Efficient cleaning: helmet cleaning can be completed in 3-8 minutes, suitable for different customer time requirements
2. Deep sterilization : Fusing ultraviolet, plasma, and disinfectant triple sterilization to kill 99% of bacteria and viruses inside the helmet
3. Intelligent and convenient : fully automatic operation, equipped with voice guidance and text prompts, can be used without professional training
4. Comfortable experience: After cleaning, the helmet has no odor and can retain its fragrance for 48 hours, solving the problem of moisture and odor inside the helmet
Application Scenario
In the ski resort, tourists who have rented helmets scan the code to start, and the cleaning machine sprays disinfectant mist to clean the lining; On the street side, cyclists stop and put their helmets in, and can take away their clean helmets in a moment; When commuters are in a hurry, the office lobby can quickly complete helmet cleaning.
