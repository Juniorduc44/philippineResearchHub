Technical highlights
1. Negative pressure dust removal: The top blower and helmet internal axial flow fan form negative pressure, thoroughly sucking away surface dust and internal dandruff, and collecting them in the bottom dust collection box
2. Atomization disinfection: After the disinfectant is atomized by the heating element, it is sprayed into the inside of the helmet through a high-pressure nozzle to clean the deep gaps that cannot be touched by hand
3. Constant temperature drying: Equipped with a temperature controller, it automatically stops when the temperature reaches the standard and restarts when it falls below the threshold, ensuring constant temperature drying within 1 minute to avoid fabric damage
4. Safety protection: Real time monitoring with infrared sensing. If any foreign objects such as arms or helmets enter the work compartment, the equipment will immediately stop running to ensure safe use
