The A026 helmet cleaning machine is equipped with two buttons corresponding to the cleaning, deodorizing, and drying functions of the upper and lower layers, respectively. Each layer can operate independently, meaning the first customer can place a helmet in the upper layer and complete the payment, then press the start button to begin the process. During this time, a second customer can place a helmet in the lower layer, complete the payment, and press the start button to initiate the cleaning process. This design ensures each layer operates independently, reducing wait times, enhancing efficiency, and improving customer satisfaction.
Our machine uses a single mainboard to control both helmet cleaning chambers and can be equipped with our mobile payment module. This means only one payment module is needed to handle transactions for both layers. Each customer pays separately for using the upper or lower layer, and the single mainboard efficiently manages payments for both chambers.
