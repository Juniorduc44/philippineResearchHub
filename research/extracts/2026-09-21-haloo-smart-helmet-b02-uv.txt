Product About Helmet cleaning vending machine
24-hour unmanned helmet cleaning solution designed for cycling scenarios
In today's rapidly developing shared riding, electric vehicles, motorcycles, and food delivery industries, helmet hygiene issues are becoming a growing concern for more and more operators and consumers. Traditional manual cleaning has low efficiency, serious odor residue, and difficult to unify hygiene standards, while ordinary simple cleaning equipment is prone to problems such as incomplete disinfection, equipment rusting, and poor outdoor durability.
In response to these industry pain points, Haloo has launched a new smart helmet cleaning vending machine, which provides a more intelligent, efficient, and hygienic helmet cleaning method for scenic spots, commercial streets, parking lots, shared riding stations, motorcycle clubs, and other scenarios through an integrated solution of "cleaning+disinfection+deodorization+drying+unmanned self-service operation".

Why are more and more operators deploying helmet cleaning vending machines?
For shared helmet operators, the biggest concern for users is usually not whether they have helmets, but rather:
• Is the helmet hygienic?
• Is there any odor?
• Are there any residual sweat and bacteria?
• Can it be rapidly recycled?
• Can the equipment operate stably outdoors for a long time?
If these issues cannot be resolved, both the user repurchase rate and brand reputation will be affected.
Haloo smart helmet cleaning vending machine is deeply developed around these core operational challenges, which not only enhances user experience but also helps operators improve equipment utilization and profitability.
Core advantages of the product
1. Outdoor three proof body design, more durable and anti-aging
Adopting a thickened sheet metal body structure, combined with high anti-corrosion spray painting technology and thickened flash film, it can operate stably even in outdoor high-temperature, humid or high-frequency environments for a long time.
The body sticker is made of outdoor three proof material:
• Anti fading
• Anti aging
• Anti curling edge
Compared to ordinary devices that are prone to fading, rusting, and aging in appearance, Haloo helmet cleaning vending machines can maintain high-end visual effects for a long time, effectively enhancing the overall image of commercial scenes.
2. Stainless steel bracket bottom plate design, more hygienic and easy to clean
Traditional helmet cleaning equipment commonly suffers from problems such as bottom fluid accumulation, difficulty in cleaning, and easy growth of bacteria.
Haloo adopts a stainless steel bracket bottom plate structure:
• Rust-resistant
• No liquid residue left
• Cleaning and maintenance are simpler
• The helmet does not directly touch the bottom
This suspended design not only improves drying efficiency, but also avoids contact between the helmet and damp surfaces, further enhancing the hygiene level.
3. Plant antibacterial fragrance system, making the helmet fresher and more comfortable
Ordinary helmets often have residual odors after cleaning, and strong chemical odors can easily cause users to dislike them.
Haloo supports air catalytic atomization liquid cleaning and offers multiple fragrance options, which can be synchronized during the cleaning process
• Antibacterial
• D eodorize
• Fresh and lingering fragrance
Users can feel a more comfortable and advanced experience when wearing it.
4. UVC LED 360 ° ultraviolet disinfection, with an antibacterial rate of over 99%
Health and safety are the core of shared helmet operation.
The device is equipped with a built-in UVC LED 360 ° ultraviolet disinfection system, which can achieve rapid and deep sterilization, effectively covering complex areas inside the helmet.
Core features:
• 360 ° ultraviolet penetration
• Quick disinfection
• Antibacterial rate >99%
• Reduce the risk of bacterial residue
Compared with the traditional simple spray cleaning method, Haloo really realizes "deep sanitary cleaning".
